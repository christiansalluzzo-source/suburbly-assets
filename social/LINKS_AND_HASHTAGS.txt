WHERE LINKS WORK
  Facebook posts ........ clickable  -> tracked website link
  X posts ............... clickable  -> tracked website link
  Instagram captions .... NOT clickable -> "Link in bio"
  Instagram Stories ..... link sticker -> tracked website link, utm_source=instagram
  YouTube Shorts ........ NOT clickable -> "Get Suburbly on Google Play: link on the channel"
  YouTube long video .... clickable in description -> tracked Play link, utm_source=youtube
  TikTok ................ no link (account under 1,000 followers) -> "Search Suburbly on Google Play"
