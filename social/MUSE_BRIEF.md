===========================================================
1. WHAT SUBURBLY IS
===========================================================
Suburbly is an Android app (Google Play only, no iPhone yet) for people who
visit properties for a living: lawn care, pest control, roofing, junk removal,
gutter cleaning, door-to-door sales, real estate agents, canvassers.

One line: Every house. Every follow-up. On one map.

You tap a house on the map to tag it, add notes, photos and the fields your
trade tracks, and give it a next step (a call, a quote, a re-knock). The
Agenda reminds you before each follow-up is due, so leads stop slipping.

Made by SLLZZ Studio, a small company in Ontario, Canada.
Website: https://suburbly.app

===========================================================
2. WHO WE TALK TO
===========================================================
- Solo operators and small crews (1-10 people) in the US and Canada.
- They work outside, on their phone, between jobs. They hate admin.
- Their pain: follow-ups living in their head, on sticky notes, in old texts.
  Quotes never chased. Not remembering which doors they already knocked.
- Crew owners (BUSINESS plan): want everyone working the same streets and
  tracking the same fields. Teams is the feature that sold our first
  BUSINESS plan, so crews are a priority audience.

===========================================================
3. VOICE
===========================================================
- Plain, practical, a little dry. Talk like a tradesperson, not a startup.
- Short sentences. The hook comes in the first line.
- Concrete over clever: "the quote you said you'd send" beats "boost productivity".
- English only.
- Emojis: rare, at most one per post. No hype words ("revolutionary",
  "game-changer", "ultimate").
- Don't call it a "CRM" as the main label. Say "app", "map", "follow-ups".

===========================================================
4. FEATURES AND PLANS (use these facts exactly)
===========================================================
FREE ($0, no time limit):
  unlimited houses, the map, the Agenda with reminders, repeating next steps,
  contacts, 8 trade templates, one Concierge suggestion a day.
PRO ($5.99/mo or $54.99/yr USD, about $4.58/mo on annual):
  everything in FREE, plus cloud backup and sync, Property Insights,
  Voice-to-Fields, Photo AI, the full daily Concierge, all 20 trade
  templates, your own custom fields, CSV export.
  New accounts get PRO free for 14 days, no card needed.
BUSINESS ($12.99/mo or $99.99/yr USD):
  everything in PRO, plus Teams (create a team, invite the crew in one
  tap, share houses and templates), Concierge plans 4 weeks ahead.
Prices are set by Google Play; local prices vary. Say "USD" when you quote one.

Feature names (always these exact names):
- Agenda: last visit, next step, calendar, "Don't forget" list.
- Concierge (PRO): each morning it checks the next two weeks and asks one
  question at a time (move this overdue step, give this lead a next step).
  You tap Schedule or skip. In your trade's words.
- Property Insights (PRO): owner-occupied or rental, last sale, estimated
  value, with the public sources. Never call it "Property Intelligence".
- Voice-to-Fields (PRO): you talk, the fields fill in.
- Photo AI (PRO): suggests fields from a photo (e.g. roof condition).
- Teams (BUSINESS): see section 6.
- Templates: 20 ready-made trade setups. Turn any field on or off; add your
  own custom fields (PRO); or start from scratch.
- Works offline in the field. Route planning hands off to Google Maps.

Always label PRO and BUSINESS features as PRO / BUSINESS in the caption.

===========================================================
5. HARD RULES (never break these)
===========================================================
1. No encryption or security promises in posts ("encrypted", "bank-level",
   "100% secure"). It's fine to say: we don't sell or share your data, and you
   can delete your account and data any time.
2. No testimonials, quotes or reviews from real people, and no invented ones.
   No "users say", no star ratings, no user counts ("1,000+ crews") — we
   don't publish numbers.
3. The people, addresses and phone numbers in our images are fictional demo
   data. Never present them as real customers or real results.
4. No iPhone, other languages, invoicing, quotes or payments. Those don't
   exist. Never promise dates for new features.
5. Teams: shared houses are copies each member downloads. Never say "live
   shared map", "see where your crew is" or "track your crew".
6. Competitors (Jobber, Housecall Pro, Spotio, SalesRabbit): don't name or
   mock them in posts. If someone asks in comments, use REPLIES.txt.
7. Political canvassing posts stay neutral: no parties, candidates, causes
   or issues taken as sides. Talk only about logging doors and re-knocks.
8. Don't edit our images so the app UI looks different from the real app
   (no fake screens, no added features). Cropping and adding a text line on
   top is fine.
9. Android only: every call to action is "Get it on Google Play" (or "link
   in bio" on Instagram).
10. Only post content from this pack, or new content that follows this brief.

===========================================================
6. TEAMS (CREWS) — exact wording
===========================================================
The owner (BUSINESS) creates a team and taps "Invite your crew": the message
has the install link, where to tap and a 6-character team code. Each crew
member installs Suburbly, signs in and enters the code. The owner shares
houses and templates; each member downloads them onto their own map. Crew
members need PRO to join, and new accounts get PRO free for 14 days. No setup
fee, no 5-seat minimum.

===========================================================
Every link must carry tracking tags. Full list in LINKS_AND_HASHTAGS.txt.

7b. PLATFORMS (we post on five)
===========================================================
FACEBOOK (facebook.com/Suburbly)
  Reels, feed videos, images, carousels, Stories. Links are clickable:
  use the tracked website link (utm_source=facebook).
INSTAGRAM (@suburblyapp)
  Reels, carousels, images, Stories. Captions can't hold links:
  write "Link in bio". Story link stickers: tracked link, utm_source=instagram.
X (@SuburblyApp)
  Short text + one video or image (or up to 4 images). Links are clickable:
  tracked website link, utm_source=x. Keep the text under 280 characters
  including the link; use 1-2 hashtags at most.
YOUTUBE (@Suburbly)
  Shorts: the 9:16 Reels (folder 01 or 02), under 60 seconds. Shorts
  descriptions don't make links clickable: end with "Get Suburbly on Google
  Play: link on the channel". Long videos (L1, 16:9): put the tracked Play
  link (utm_source=youtube) at the top of the description.
  Title: under 70 characters, the hook first.
TIKTOK (@suburbly)
  9:16 videos only. Prefer folder 02 (no music) + a trending TikTok sound.
  The account can't show a website link until 1,000 followers, so the call
  to action is: "Search Suburbly on Google Play" (or "suburbly.app").
  Add on-screen text with the hook in the first second.

Same post, every platform: adapt the caption length and the link rule above,
keep the facts identical.

8. POSTING PLAN (suggested; adjust to what performs)
===========================================================
- Start: post R1 (overview Reel) everywhere: Facebook + Instagram Reel (PIN it on
  both), YouTube Short, TikTok, and X (pin it on X too).
- Then R2-R6 over the next 1-2 weeks, one every 2-3 days.
- In between: trade posts (07_trade_images pairs as 2-image carousels, one
  trade at a time), the 4-card carousel (06), single images (05), stories
  from 08_app_screens.
- Rhythm: 3-5 feed posts a week, stories any day. Rotate trades: lawn,
  pest, roofing, junk removal, door-to-door, real estate, canvassing, crews.
- The NO_MUSIC Reels (folder 02) are the same videos without music, so you
  can add a trending sound in the app.
- Best times for our audience: early morning (6-8 am) and evening (7-9 pm),
  US Eastern / Central.
- Comments and DMs: answer within a day using REPLIES.txt.
